Every feature, explained

AI Context Bridge does two things: it moves content from a source into a destination AI, and it adds small extras once you're already talking to Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini.

Content handoff

Reddit thread

Copying a Reddit post plus its comments normally means losing the formatting along the way. This adds a menu right on the thread page: pick a destination, and the whole thing arrives in a new tab, already formatted.

Claude ChatGPT Gemini Clipboard

Medium article

Same problem, same fix, for Medium. Whatever you can read on the page gets sent through, with no copy-paste and no stripped formatting.

Claude ChatGPT Gemini Clipboard

ChatGPT conversation

Want a second opinion, or to keep going in Claude without retyping everything? This reads your ChatGPT conversation and hands it to Claude: summarized and continued, or reviewed for a second opinion.

Claude

On-platform extras

Time Awareness

An AI chat has no built-in sense of elapsed time: walk away for 3 hours or 3 days and the next message looks identical to it either way. This tells your AI the real gap, so it can account for it instead of pretending no time passed.

Claude ChatGPT Gemini

Ambient Sounds

A text-only chat can feel like talking into silence. This adds a subtle audio presence on Claude.ai: a sense that something's actually alive on the other end, not just text on a screen.

Claude only

Default Model

Picking a model on every handoff gets old fast. Set your preferred Claude model once in the popup, and every handoff opens directly there from then on.

Claude only
