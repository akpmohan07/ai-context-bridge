AI Context Bridge
How it works Features GitHub

Stop copy-pasting into your AI chat.

AI Context Bridge is a free, open-source browser extension for Chrome, Edge, and Firefox that sends a Reddit thread, a Medium article, or a ChatGPT conversation straight into Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini, in one click, with nothing lost in the paste.

How it works

1

Browse normally

Reading a Reddit thread, a Medium article, or chatting in ChatGPT.

2

Click the button it adds

No selecting text, no copying, no switching tabs to paste.

3

Land in your AI chat

The content's already there, formatted, in a new tab, ready for your question.

The extras that come along for free

Time Awareness

An AI chat has no idea how long you were gone: 3 hours or 3 days look identical to it. Time Awareness tells it the real gap, so it stops pretending no time passed. Works on Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini.

Ambient Sounds

A text-only chat can feel like talking into silence. On Claude.ai, a subtle ambient sound gives a sense that something's actually alive on the other end.

Default Model

Picking a model on every single handoff gets old fast. Set yours once on Claude.ai, and every handoff opens there automatically.

Why trust it

"Everything happens locally, in your browser, on your device. There's no server, so there's nowhere for your data to go except your own browser and the AI tab you chose to open."

Frequently asked questions

Is AI Context Bridge free?

Yes. No catch, no premium tier, no account required. "Free" extensions often make it up by monetizing your data instead; this one doesn't, and because it's open-source under the Apache 2.0 license, you can verify that yourself instead of taking it on faith.

Which AI platforms does it work with?

All three of the major ones (Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini), so you're never locked into whichever AI you happened to open first. Sources are Reddit threads, Medium articles, and ChatGPT conversations.

Does AI Context Bridge collect my data?

No, and that's not just a policy, it's architectural: there's no server for your data to go to. It runs entirely locally in your browser, with no analytics or tracking. Page content is only read when you click the extension's button, and it's used only to hand off to the AI chat you choose.

What is Time Awareness?

Time Awareness tells your AI how much real time passed since your last message. Without it, a 3-hour gap and a 3-day gap look identical to an AI chat. Works on Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini.

Is AI Context Bridge open source?

Yes, so you don't have to take the privacy claims above on faith. Every line is public on GitHub under the Apache 2.0 license; you can verify exactly what the code does.
